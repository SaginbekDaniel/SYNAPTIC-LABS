## Protocol

* Matching: An item matches the planted scenario containing its subject account with the largest account overlap, requiring at least min(2, item accounts) shared accounts.
* Flagged: NEXORA alert with band medium, high or critical; insufficient_evidence alerts are 'surfaced'.
* Unit — **scenario**: Planted network. Precision, recall, F1, FPR, FNR count scenarios only.
* Unit — **alert**: Review item. Alert precision = flagged items that match a suspicious scenario.
* Unit — **queue**: Per-dataset analyst queue in review order; P@K and Recall@K averaged over datasets.

## Limitations

* All datasets are synthetic and generated by the same team that wrote the detectors; typologies, parameter ranges and hard negatives reflect our assumptions, not observed bank data.
* Scenario counts per split are small (tens per family); differences of one or two scenarios are noise.
* Priority is a triage ranking, not a calibrated probability of fraud.
* Latency is wall-clock time of the pure analysis pipeline on one CPU core of the benchmark machine; it excludes database I/O and is not a banking-scale throughput claim.
* Legitimate hubs capped by peer counter-evidence remain alerted at medium by design; any-alert FPR therefore stays high while high-band FPR measures the prioritisation effect.
